Why this exists

Nordic balancing prices come from eSett, Energinet’s Energi Data Service, Fingrid, the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform and Svenska kraftnät. Their APIs return only the current value: when a price is revised, the earlier value is gone. A collector polls the imbalance prices every 15 minutes and the reserve capacity prices hourly, and keeps every version it sees.

That record makes it possible to show when each price was first published and how often it was revised afterwards — from the day collection started, not from the day a provider happened to freeze its numbers. The site is built on the open-source nordic-balancing Python library.
