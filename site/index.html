When Nordic balancing prices appear, and how often they change

The same electricity prices are published by several organisations, at different times, and are sometimes changed afterwards. Their websites only show the latest number. This site checks them every 15 minutes and keeps every version, so you can see who publishes first and what changed later.

What is an imbalance price?

Power companies promise in advance how much they will produce or use. Whoever ends up off that plan pays or is paid the imbalance price for each 15 minutes. It is only known after those 15 minutes are over.

Why does the timing matter?

Traders, battery operators and balance responsible parties act on this price. Knowing where it appears first, and how long it usually takes, tells them when they can rely on it.

Why do later changes matter?

If a published price is changed, anyone who acted on the first number acted on a wrong one. Analyses built on today’s final numbers quietly assume they were known sooner, and more accurately, than they were.

      Data & method

      How it is measured, and how to use the data

      First publication

      A price counts as published at the first check that returns it with a value. Rows that appear without a price are not yet published.

      Imbalance prices are checked every 15 minutes and reserve capacity every hour, so a publication time is accurate to within one check.

      What is not counted

      Values already on record when collection started, or first seen after a collector outage, have no reliable publication time. They are left out of the delays and of the share changed.

      Statistics cover the last 7 days; the daily view covers closed UTC days.

      Revisions

      Any change to a published value is a revision, and so is a value that disappears. The log keeps the earlier and the new price. Nothing is smoothed or corrected, and a 0.00 is shown as published.

      Files and fields

      The page reads two JSON files, refreshed every 15 minutes. Use them directly, or download any table above as CSV.
