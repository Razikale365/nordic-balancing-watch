Changes to published figures

Every correction to the figures on this site: what was wrong, which figures it affected, how it was corrected and what now prevents it. Collected values are never edited or deleted. A value found to come from a collection fault is marked with the reason and left out of the figures.

28 October 2026: revision counts, withdrawals and Danish placeholders

Period affected: start of collection (24 October 2026, 07:34 UTC) to 28 October 2026, 16:40 UTC. Figures were recomputed on 28 October 2026.

1. Revision counts included changes to non-price fields

The summary counted a row as revised whenever any stored field changed, such as satisfied demand, a direction or a capacity volume, not only when a price changed. This affected the revision counts and the share of prices changed later. The revision log was not affected: it always compared prices.

2. Withdrawals that were not withdrawals

No source withdrew a published value in this period, but two collection faults recorded withdrawals:

  • On 26 October 2026 at 10:30 UTC, ENTSO-E answered one request without any NO2 rows. The collector read the empty zone as 189 withdrawn values, which came back at the next check.
  • The daily eSett query lost the interval on a 31-day query boundary when the window started a few seconds past the quarter hour, a fault in nordic-balancing 1.0.0rc1 fixed in 1.0.0rc2. Each day this recorded 12 withdrawals, one per zone, re-added the next day: 48 in all.

The 189 ENTSO-E entries and 36 eSett entries are marked. The last 12 eSett entries are marked once their interval is returned again; the rule requires the same interval to come back, so a real withdrawal stays visible.

3. Danish eSett placeholders read as prices

Shortly after each interval, eSett publishes a provisional row for DK1 and DK2 with every price at 0.00 before the real price. nordic-balancing 1.0.0rc1 treated such a row as not yet published only when its direction was 0, so placeholders with an up or down direction were read as a settled price of 0.00. It also hid three real settled prices of 0.00 (DK1, 25 October 2026, 01:15 to 01:45 UTC, when the spot price was also 0.00).

For 132 Danish intervals the eSett first-publication time was too early and the real price was counted as a revision. nordic-balancing 1.0.0rc3 recognises the placeholder by its non-zero spot difference (imblSpotDifferencePrice), checked against every Danish row from 2025 to October 2026 and against Energi Data Service. The collector switched to it on 28 October 2026 at 16:17 UTC; the 132 placeholders, and 2 rows re-read at the switch, are marked.

4. Danish-krone recalculations counted as revisions

Energi Data Service recalculates its Danish-krone imbalance price from the euro price once a day, around 16:30 UTC, moving it by a few øre while the euro price stays the same. These 268 recalculations (125 DK1, 143 DK2) were counted as revisions. No euro imbalance price from Energi Data Service was revised. They are now reported separately as currency recalculations.

5. Finnish eSett prices first published as 0.00

About two hours after an interval ends, eSett publishes the Finnish imbalance price as 0.00. Roughly an hour later it replaces that with the settled price. Fingrid and ENTSO-E have the settled price from the start. Each 0.00 was counted as eSett's first publication and the settled price as a revision: 176 of the 178 Finnish eSett revisions up to 28 October 2026, 19:11 UTC. Sweden, Norway and Denmark show no such rows.

These 0.00 rows are now marked as they are replaced, every 15 minutes. A 0.00 is marked only when eSett later replaced it and Fingrid never showed 0.00 for that interval, so a real price of 0.00 (76 in the collected data, matched by Fingrid and ENTSO-E) stays.

Two of the Finnish changes are genuine corrections and stay as revisions: 25 October 2026, 00:00 UTC, from −15.77 to −2.35 (first in Fingrid, then ENTSO-E, then eSett), and 16 October 2026, 02:45 UTC, from −79.60 to −1.00 (eSett and ENTSO-E).

What now prevents a repeat

  • A value counts as withdrawn only when two successful checks in a row miss it.
  • A zone missing from an otherwise successful answer is never read as withdrawals.
  • Every check records the version of nordic-balancing that read it.
  • For DK1, DK2 and Finnish eSett rows, every distinct row the source sent is kept, so a reading can be checked against the original.
  • A fix to nordic-balancing is checked against the collected data and applied to the collector the same day.
  • Automated tests cover each case above.
